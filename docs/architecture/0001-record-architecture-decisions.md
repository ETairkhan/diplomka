# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

The team needs to remember why important technical choices were made,
and the diploma defense needs this explanation.

## Decision

We write an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) for every important decision
and keep them in `docs/architecture/`.

## Consequences

Decisions are easy to explain and review. New ADRs take a few minutes to write.
